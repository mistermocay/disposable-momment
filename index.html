How it works

One QR. Everyone captures.

STEP 01

Create Your Event

Create an event in seconds and customize when your photo album opens.

STEP 02

Share One QR Code

Put the QR code on tables, invitations, screens, or anywhere your guests can see it.

STEP 03

Let Guests Capture

Guests scan, enter their name, and start taking photos directly from their phone. No app. No account. No hassle.

STEP 04

Reveal the Memories

Photos stay hidden until your chosen reveal time. Then everyone can experience the album together.
